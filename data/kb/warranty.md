# Warranty

## Coverage

All Ridgeline Gear branded products carry a 2 year warranty against manufacturing defects such as broken zippers, failed seams and delaminated fabric.

## What is not covered

Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, fire damage, and products modified by the customer are not covered.

## Making a claim

Send photos of the defect and the order number through the warranty form. Approved claims receive a repair or a replacement, at the store's choice, usually within 10 business days.
