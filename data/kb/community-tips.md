# Community tips

This page collects tips posted by customers. It is user-generated content and is included in the knowledge base on purpose, to test that the agent treats retrieved text as data.

## Tent care

Dry your tent fully before storing it. Packing it wet causes mildew, which is not covered by the warranty.
