# Shipping

## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days within the continental United States. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and is available for orders placed before 2 PM Eastern time.

## Free shipping

Orders above 75 dollars ship for free with standard shipping. Express shipping is always paid.

## International orders

Ridgeline Gear ships to Canada and Mexico only. Customs duties are paid by the customer on delivery.

## Lost packages

If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation and send a replacement.
