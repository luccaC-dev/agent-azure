# Account and security

## Password reset

Customers can reset their password from the sign in page using the "Forgot password" link. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

## Two factor authentication

Two factor authentication can be turned on in Account Settings, under Security. Authenticator apps are supported; SMS codes are not.

## Deleting an account

Account deletion requests are processed within 14 days. Order history is kept for 5 years for tax reasons even after the account is deleted.
