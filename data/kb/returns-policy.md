# Returns policy

Ridgeline Gear is a fictional outdoor equipment store used for this demo.

## Return window

Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging with tags attached.

## Used items

Tents, sleeping bags and backpacks that have been used outdoors cannot be returned, but they are still covered by the warranty if they fail because of a manufacturing defect.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse inspects the item. Store credit is issued immediately if the customer prefers it.
